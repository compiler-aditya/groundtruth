# Site notes (design intent)

## Block A
Fixed-tilt array, 8 rows of 24 modules (192 modules). All rows to be fully populated before mechanical completion.

## Block C
Rows C1-C7, each split by the cable trench into two tables (14 tables). Modules two high in landscape.

## Agrivoltaic test rows
Heggelbach pilot: one elevated row of module strips on a steel frame above cropland, with a gravel farm track
along one edge. Aasen plant: vertical bifacial modules in fence-like rows, two modules high per bay, with grass
maintained between the rows; the site borders a highway.

## Equipment yard and substation
Motors, the cast-resin transformer TX-1 and current transformer CT-1 per the equipment schedule. Every unit's
nameplate must match its schedule row before energisation.
